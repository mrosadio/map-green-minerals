# Data notice

The partnership data in this folder (`bilateralPartner.json`, `multiPartner.json`,
`numberPartner.json`, `partnerNarratives.json`) was collected and compiled by the
Africa Policy Research Institute (APRI), Geopolitics and Geoeconomics Program.

**All rights reserved.** This data is not released under an open licence. It is
included in this repository only so the interactive map can be demonstrated.
Do not copy, redistribute or reuse it without written permission from APRI.

The map reflects the research as of October 2026 and is not exhaustive.

The geometry files are not APRI data and are not covered by this notice:

- `world.geojson` comes from the [D3 Graph Gallery](https://github.com/holtzy/D3-graph-gallery)
  repository by Yan Holtz, which is published under the MIT licence.
- `eu.geojson` is derived from `world.geojson`, filtered to EU member states.
